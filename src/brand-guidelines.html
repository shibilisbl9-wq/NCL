About this book

These guidelines describe the New City Light identity as delivered in the rebranding files, and set out the stationery system built from it: letterhead, envelopes, quotation, stamp, plastic bag and email signature.

Where a value was measured from existing artwork rather than specified by the client (slate colour, pattern, text-safe orange), the page says so.

Who we are

New City Light (NCL) is a Baghdad lighting company that designs, supplies, installs and commissions lighting for homes, commercial buildings and large public projects.

What we do

  • 1 · Study & design. Lighting design and illumination calculations for every kind of project, to international standards.
  • 2 · Supply. Quality lighting products and electrical installations, with genuine warranties.
  • 3 · Installation. Specialised technical crews, from residential work to complex media façades and architectural lighting.
  • 4 · Programming & commissioning. Smart control systems set up for the best aesthetic and functional result.

Partners named in the company brochure

Exclusive agent in Iraq for DuLight (UAE) and for international brands including OSRAM, Tridonic, LEDVANCE, PCE and Frequentis.

Milestones

2004Becomes distributor for OSRAM (Germany) and Tridonic (Austria).
2005Opens a branch in Dubai to serve the Iraqi market with high-quality materials and technology transfer.
2012Establishes a factory in the Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), Dubai, to manufacture and assemble lighting fixtures.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep a margin equal to x, the height of the capital letters in the wordmark, clear on all sides. Below the minimum widths, switch to the symbol alone.

Why 40 mm?

The tagline is the first element to break up. Below about 40 mm its capitals fall under 2 pt and fill in when printed. These minimums are our recommendation; confirm on a printed proof.

  • Never place text or graphics inside the clear space.
  • Align the logo to the page margin, not to an arbitrary position.

Rules of thumb

  • Text on an orange surface: navy. The white logo on orange is the one exception, because it is artwork.
  • Orange type on white: only for headlines at 18 pt bold or larger. Otherwise use Orange Text #BD470B.
  • Footer and caption text on navy: off-white at 75% or stronger.
  • Never put orange text on slate below 14 pt (3.6 : 1).

Ratios calculated from the hex values on the palette page. WCAG AA requires 4.5 : 1 for normal text and 3 : 1 for large text.

Symbol & pattern

The symbol can be enlarged and cropped as a graphic device. The pattern is a quiet, tone-on-tone texture made of the same swept petal, used to give flat colour some depth.

Symbol cropped · navy
Pattern · on orange
Pattern · on slate
Pattern · on navy

Symbol device

  • Crop at the page edge, never inside a box.
  • One large symbol per layout.
  • Orange on navy, white on orange, navy on white.

Pattern

  • Contrast between pattern and ground stays below about 11%.
  • Never behind running text; use on bands, flaps and closed surfaces.
  • Files: brand/patterns/*.svg (seamless tile).

Note

The pattern tile here is rebuilt from the approved card and envelope mockups. If the original pattern artwork exists, drop it into brand/patterns and rebuild: nothing else changes.

04 · Graphic language

Imagery

Night, architecture, warm light. Photography should feel like the moment a space comes alive: deep navy shadows, glowing amber light, clean geometry.

Existing campaign visuals supplied with the project.

Do

  • Dusk and night scenes, light as the hero.
  • Warm light (about 2700–3000 K) against deep blue-black shadow.
  • Clean architecture; one clear subject per frame.
  • Leave calm space for headline and logo.

Don’t

  • Flat daylight stock photography or cold, clinical white light.
  • Cluttered frames where the logo has to fight the picture.
  • Heavy filters, clip-art, or lens-flare effects.
  • Placing the logo directly on bright, busy areas; use a navy overlay first.

To confirm before printing

  • Logo files. In the supplied full-colour and single-colour PNGs the ® ring is a hairline displaced to the right of the “R”. This pack rebuilds it from the correct on-dark file. Please fix the master artwork.
  • Website. The brochure says dulights.com (also its email); the business card mockup says www.NewCityLight.com. One has to win.
  • Arabic spelling. The Arabic logo reads «انارة» (no hamza); the brochure body text writes «إنارة». Decide once and use it everywhere.
  • Experience claim. The brochure says “more than 30 years” but its timeline starts in 1998 (28 years). Don’t print either on stationery until settled.
  • Fonts. GE SS Two and Nasalization are commercial or personal-use only; check licences. Montserrat and Noto Kufi are free.
  • Colours. CMYK values are starting points. Approve a printed swatch (and Pantone match if you use spot colour).
  • Pattern. Rebuilt from the mockups. Replace with the original if one exists.
  • Symbol likeness. Alone, the symbol can read like other orange “spark” marks used by tech brands. Navy icon adopted; colour options and symbol concepts (including a geometric direction) are in 08-Symbol-Concepts for NCL to review.
  • Quotation terms & stamp wording. Placeholders. Legal and registration wording needs NCL’s sign-off.
