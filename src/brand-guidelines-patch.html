About this book

These guidelines describe the New City Light identity as delivered in the rebranding files, and set out the stationery system built from it: letterhead, envelopes, quotation, stamp, plastic bag and email signature.

Where a value was measured from existing artwork rather than specified by the client (slate colour, pattern, text-safe orange), the page says so.

Master · 1800 × 621 px · default text

Layers in the editable files

Background hero (photo) · white panel · footer strip (image) · logo (vector) · tagline · name & title · contacts (icons + text) · company name & address. Only the two images are raster; the rest is vector or live text.

Size1800 × 621 px master, inserted at 600 px wide: 3× for sharp screens
TypeMontserrat; the Arabic tagline in Noto Kufi Arabic. The approved JPG set name and address in another sans; the editable files use Montserrat
EditableSVG with layers and live text (Illustrator, Figma, Inkscape) · .ai, PDF-compatible, live text · PSD, one layer per element, text rasterised
EditorNCL-Email-Signature-Editor.html: type name, title and contacts, download PNG, JPG or SVG. Works offline, fonts embedded
Clickable textAn image alone is not clickable, stays hidden until the reader allows images and is silent to screen readers: always type name, phone and email as text under it
Fonts to installbrand/fonts/desktop/ · Montserrat and Noto Kufi Arabic TTF, free (SIL OFL), needed to edit the .ai and SVG

To confirm before printing

  • Logo master. The English lockup is now the vector artwork from NCL’s SAMP file (Oct 2026), with the tagline respaced to the wordmark width and the text centred on the symbol. Use these files from now on; the older PNGs are retired.
  • Website. The brochure says dulights.com (also its email); the business card mockup says www.NewCityLight.com. One has to win.
  • Arabic spelling. The Arabic logo reads «انارة» (no hamza); the brochure body text writes «إنارة». Decide once and use it everywhere.
  • Experience claim. The brochure says “more than 30 years” but its timeline starts in 1998 (28 years). Don’t print either on stationery until settled.
  • Fonts. GE SS Two and Nasalization are commercial or personal-use only; check licences. Montserrat and Noto Kufi are free.
  • Colours. CMYK values are starting points. Approve a printed swatch (and Pantone match if you use spot colour).
  • Quotation terms & stamp wording. Placeholders. Legal and registration wording needs NCL’s sign-off.
