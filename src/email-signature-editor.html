Using it in Outlook or Gmail: insert the PNG into the signature editor and set its width to 600 px (the file is 3× for sharp screens). Image-only signatures are not clickable, are hidden until the reader allows images, and are invisible to screen readers, so also type your name, phone and email as plain text under the image.

Fonts: Montserrat and Noto Kufi Arabic are embedded in this page, so the export looks the same on any computer. Long text is not shrunk automatically: keep lines about the length of the defaults.
